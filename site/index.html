Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Not as a promise in a policy: there is simply nowhere for it to go. Pladder has no server.

  • On-device. Your Mac's Neural Engine turns speech into text, and the audio is gone the moment it has.
  • Offline. Once the speech model is downloaded, it works with Wi-Fi off.
  • Nobody watching. No account, no tracking, no update check. The microphone is on only while you hold the key.
  • Checkable. It is open source, so anyone can read what it does.

How Pladder handles your words

Made for the prompt box.

Hold ⌥ Space, describe the change, tap V with the same hand and let go. The prompt is pasted, Return is pressed, and your agent is already working, while your other hand never left the mouse.

Pladder types wherever your cursor is: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, T3 Code, OpenCode, any terminal, any editor, any text field. A dictionary fixes the names speech models stumble on, so "clode code" comes out as "Claude Code", every time.

The small things, done right.

Your keys
Record any key or combination to talk and to send, or a toggle key for long dictations. Escape throws a recording away.
It learns your words
Fix a word by hand after a dictation and Pladder offers to remember it. Only when you say so.
No ums
"Uh" and "um" are dropped, and saying "comma" or "new paragraph" gives you the mark.
Polish, if you like
An optional on-device model turns "wait, no, Friday" into "Friday" before the paste.
25 languages
English, German, French, Spanish and the rest of Europe, recognised as you speak. Nothing to switch.

Questions

Is there a free offline dictation app for Mac?

Yes. Pladder is free, MIT-licensed and runs entirely on your Mac. After a one-time download of the speech model it works with Wi-Fi off. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.

How do I dictate into Claude Code?

Install Pladder, click into Claude Code's prompt, hold Option+Space, say the prompt and let go. The words are pasted at the cursor. Tap V while you still hold the keys and Pladder presses Return for you, so the prompt is sent. The same works in Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, T3 Code and every other app that takes text.

Does Pladder send my voice to the cloud?

No. Audio and text never leave the Mac. Speech is transcribed on the Neural Engine, the microphone is open only while you hold the key, and there is no account, no analytics and no update check. The only network requests are one-time model downloads from Hugging Face.

How is Pladder different from macOS Dictation and Whisper apps?

Pladder is push-to-talk: it records only while you hold the key and pastes the whole text when you let go, into any app. It runs Parakeet Ultra, a post-training of NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT v3, rather than Whisper, and transcribes while you are still speaking, so the wait after you let go stays the same for a sentence or for five minutes. It is open source, and its speed is benchmarked in the repository rather than claimed.

Which Macs and languages does Pladder support?

Any Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later; the benchmarks are taken on an M1, so every newer chip is faster. It understands the 25 European languages of Parakeet, among them English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Ukrainian, and detects the language as you speak.

Free. Really.

No trial, no subscription, no account. Pladder is open source under the MIT license.
